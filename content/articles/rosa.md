title: Rosa
description: The day the sun went dark for a second.
date: 2026-07-07
---

Today I watched the sun flicker as one if its smallest but brightest flames was extinguished. If you were outside you may have mistake it as a cloud passing overhead of missed it entirely in the blink of an eye. But the whole world dimmed—just for a moment.

The flames of our lives are connected to everyone around us. When a flame is blown out, like a candle in eternity, our world can fracture. The only reason it doesn't crumble around us is because of the memories and warmth imbued by those little flames. And so, in leaving they are the ones that give us the strength to remain.
